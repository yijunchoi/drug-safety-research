Drug safety research agent

Second Look

What does the FDA's own data say about your medication? Ask a question and a small team of AI agents digs through millions of FDA side-effect reports, official drug labels and recall notices, then writes you a short report where every number links to its source.

What it explores

Three public FDA databases through openFDA: side-effect reports (FAERS, 20M+ reports since 2004), drug labels and their warnings, and drug recalls. Medicare Part D counts how many patients take each drug, so reports become rates. NIH RxNorm turns brand names into ingredients.

Who it's for

Patients and caregivers who heard something about a drug (on the news, TikTok, a forum) and want to see what the FDA's data actually shows, and what it can't show.

How to use it

  1. Name a drug and what you want to know.
  2. If your question is broad, it asks you to narrow it.
  3. Watch the agents work, then read the cited report. Answers take 1-3 minutes.

The team

Lead plans and asks for clarification · Researchers query openFDA in parallel · Writer writes the cited report · Fact-checker checks every number before you see it

Not medical advice. FDA side-effect reports are unverified and don't prove a drug caused anything; talk to your doctor or pharmacist before changing any medication. Built for IEOR 4570 with Gemini, the OpenAI Agents SDK and openFDA.
